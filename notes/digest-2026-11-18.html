Investor takeaway: Keep new equity buying small until the S&P holds its 200-day average on a close and dealers' short gamma (about $13.3B per 1% move) expires Friday: Nvidia's beat-and-raise faded within a session, which Ree reads as selling that isn't finished.

Nvidia's Quarter: The Numbers

Investor takeaway: The business case for Nvidia got stronger: $115.8B of revenue (+103%), a $130B guide with no China and fiscal 2028 growth raised to about 75%. Size it on governance risk (theme 4), not on demand, which the report says is still ahead of supply.

  • A beat and a raise$115.8B vs. $109B; $2.68 vs. $2.47; $130B guide; FY2028 ~75%

    The headline beat (Tae Kim, Key Context; Michael Parekh, AI-RTZ #1274 and ARD #208; Sergey, Compounding Your Wealth). Revenue for the quarter ended October 25 was $115.8B, up 103% on the year and 20% on the quarter, against about $109B expected; adjusted EPS was $2.68 against $2.47; gross margin was 74.9% against 74%.

    • The guide was $130B ± 3% for the January quarter against about $125B consensus, with "no Data Center compute revenue from China" assumed. Interim CEO Colette Kress raised fiscal 2028 growth to about 75%, from 70%, "off what is now a higher fiscal 2027 base."
    • Sergey has the beat at 6.9% on revenue and 8.9% on EPS, the guide 4.0% above estimates, and revenue 7.3% above Nvidia's own guide, the largest guidance beat in eight quarters.
    • Vs. prior digests the 11-17 digest's setup was about $2.47 on about $109B with options pricing the biggest post-earnings move since February 2025; the stock reached its after-hours high during the call against an implied move of about 8% (Ree) (market-newsletter-digest-2026-11-17 §2).
    • Different measures: Tae Kim's consensus is $108.9B, Parekh's "roughly $109B"; GAAP EPS was $2.70 (QTR's figure) and non-GAAP $2.68.
